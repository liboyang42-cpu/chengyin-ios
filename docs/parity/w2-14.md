## Decisions needed

1. Invite countdown (`邀约剩 N 天有效`): the native contract keeps unzoned source wall time verbatim and computes no countdown. Choose a server time zone (Asia/Shanghai) or keep showing the raw expiry.
2. Nearby topic mode: allow `api/merchant/chapter-application/invitable` on the nearby-only transport scope (reviewed allowlist), or keep topic mode out of native.
3. Deposit default: the source shows ¥50 when `depositAmount` is missing; native shows "amount awaiting confirmation" instead of inventing a price.
4. Invite alerts (WeChat subscribe `coopInvited`): pick a native notification equivalent or drop.
