## Stamp camera (P027) — blocked

Camera execution and `/api/roam/stamp/create {picUrl, idempotencyKey}` stay off (`cameraEnabled` false, write endpoint allowlist empty). The existing coordinator already mirrors the source: purpose/consent toggle (`onPrivacyGateSettled`), `onShutter` (disabled), crop and metadata strip (`RoamStampPixelCrop`), `onRetake`, `onSave` upload → create with a stable idempotency key, pending-create recovery and an uncertain-outcome state.
Gaps needing a decision: enabling the camera and the create write; pinch-to-resize crop (`onTouchStart` / `onTouchMove`) versus the fixed 4:5 crop; an `openSetting` deep link after camera denial.
