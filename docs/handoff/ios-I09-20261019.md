## Team (lead team-progress) — fix

Mini `pages/play/index.js` `loadTeamProgress` polls every 4s while `exists`, stops on
`exists=false` and on hide/unload, and drops a response only when it is older than the
**applied** one (`_leadProgressAppliedEpoch`). iOS had a manual load only and no response ordering:
an overlapping manual load / post-command readback could let a slow earlier response overwrite
newer team state, and the leader buttons follow `lead.allows(...)`.

- `Core/PlayExperienceCoordinator.swift`: `loadLead` → `readLead` with issue/applied epochs;
  new `pollLead(context:intervalNanoseconds:sleep:)`; `leadReadFailed` (poll failure keeps phase
  and the last applied lead; manual reads and `.unauthorized` keep the existing `fail` path).
- `Core/PlayLeadProgressPolling.swift` (new): interval, stop rule, task key.
- `App/PlayLeadProgressPollingModifier.swift` (new): `.task(id:)` host, cancelled on leave,
  inactive scene or authority change. One-line hook + failure text in `App/PlayExperienceView.swift`.
- `PlayExperienceService.teamProgress` unchanged (no new fields, no requestId).

PLAYER team readback (`Core/PlayPlayerTeamReadback.swift`) needed no change: it already keeps a
revision floor and generation guard; added `testSlowEarlierRefreshCannotOverwriteNewerTeamStatus`.
