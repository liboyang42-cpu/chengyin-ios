## Changes

- `Core/PlatformAudioLifecycle.swift` (new): `PlatformAudioLifecycleSignal` and `PlatformAudioLifecyclePolicy`. These form one pure decision table:
  - Stop and release on: call, Siri or alarm, another app's recording (`interruptionBegan`); headphone unplug (`routeOldDeviceUnavailable`); other or unknown route reasons; media reset; background; and any unrecognised notification. All of these fail closed.
  - Ignore: an interruption end, **including `shouldResume`**. There is no auto-resume, so audio never restarts after a user pause, a cancel or a call. Only an explicit tap plays again.
  - Ignore only while the owner's own category is still active: `categoryChange` (the echo of its own `setCategory`) and `newDeviceAvailable` (headphones plugged in). If another category has taken over, both stop.
- `App/NativeAudioLifecycleObservation.swift` (new): maps `Notification` to a signal, and keeps one observer set per run. Calling `observe` again removes the previous set first, so observers are never registered twice. A signal already queued for an invalidated run is dropped, so an old callback cannot act on a new session.
- `NativePlatformAudioDriver`: uses the shared observation and policy. The only behaviour change is that plugging in headphones no longer stops playback.
- `MerchantNPCAVVoiceDevice`: uses the shared observation for recording and sample playback, which adds route, reset and background coverage and ignores interruption-ended. Notifications and the temp directory can now be injected. `MerchantNPCVoiceTemporaryFiles.sweep` removes `merchant-voice-*.m4a` takes older than 15 minutes before the next recording. The protocol, the defaults (still OFF) and the permission flow are unchanged.
- Contract checks:
  - `tools/check_merchant_voice_samples.py` now checks for the interruption observer in the shared helper, and also requires the device to observe, invalidate and sweep.
  - `tools/check_platform_consumers.py`: the `adapters concrete` check was already failing on the base, because it still matched the pre-refactor `AVAudioPlayer(data: data)`. It now matches the injected `makePlayer`. Two checks were added: `audio lifecycle shared` and `no background audio entitlement`.

## Tests added (deterministic, injectable notification sources)

- Core `PlatformChapterAudioTests`: lifecycle policy table; a user pause followed by an interruption releases audio and only an explicit tap restarts it; an old run's callback cannot drive a replacement, a suspended player or a disposed player.
- Core `ShopNPCInterruptionTests`: stop signals suspend the conversation; interruption-ended (`shouldResume`) and headphone plug-in never resume or dispatch; after invalidate (page exit or account switch), resume does nothing.
- AppUnit `NativeAudioLifecycleTests`: headphone plug-in keeps playing; unplug stops; a later ended+shouldResume does not resume. `newDeviceAvailable` under a foreign category stops. Repeated starts keep exactly one observer set, and dispose releases all of them.
- AppUnit `NativeAudioLifecycleObservationTests` (new file): notification→signal mapping; re-observing replaces the set and drops the old run's queued signal; invalidate drops pending signals.
- AppUnit `MerchantNPCVoiceDeviceLifecycleTests`: an idle device holds no observers; the sweep removes only stale `merchant-voice-*.m4a` files.

## Real-iPhone verification checklist (for the user)

Use a build where the chapter, narration or template audio and the merchant voice samples are explicitly enabled for testing. Both stay OFF in shipped config. For every step also check that the UI state matches what you hear.

1. **Incoming call while narration plays:** audio stops and the control shows the idle/play state. Decline or end the call: audio does **not** restart by itself. Tap play: it starts from the beginning.
2. **Call while narration is paused by the user:** after the call it stays silent, and the control shows idle (not playing).
3. **Siri or an alarm during playback:** same as 1.
4. **Wired or Bluetooth headphones unplugged during playback:** stops immediately, with nothing coming from the speaker. Reconnecting does not resume.
5. **Headphones plugged in during playback:** playback continues, now in the headphones.
6. **Lock screen during playback:** audio stops (there is no background mode). Unlock: still stopped and needs a new tap. No lock-screen Now Playing controls appear.
7. **Home / app switcher during playback:** stops. Return to the app: stopped, and you have to approve the media again and tap play.
8. **Another app starts recording (e.g. Voice Memos) while ours plays:** ours stops and does not come back when the other app finishes.
9. **Merchant voice sample recording:** during a take, (a) a call, (b) unplugging the headset, and (c) backgrounding each discard the take and show it as interrupted. Afterwards there is no `merchant-voice-*.m4a` in the app's tmp (check via the Xcode container download). The red microphone indicator turns off.
10. **Merchant sample playback:** an unplug or a call stops it, and it does not resume.
11. **Page exit:** leaving the chapter, template or merchant page mid-play stops the audio. Other apps' audio (e.g. Music) is allowed to resume, because we deactivate with `notifyOthersOnDeactivation`.
12. **Account switch during playback:** audio stops. After signing in as another account, nothing from the old session plays, even if a slow download finishes late.
13. **Repeat steps 1, 4 and 7 three times in a row:** each event stops playback exactly once, with no double toggles, crashes or stuck "loading" state. This checks that observers are not duplicated.
14. **Kill the app mid-recording, relaunch, record again:** the old orphaned take is swept once it is older than 15 minutes.
