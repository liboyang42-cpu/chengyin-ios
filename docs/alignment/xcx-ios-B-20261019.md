# 小程序 ↔ iOS 对齐（B 区）· 2026-10-19

The mini-program is the product source of truth: `liboyang42-cpu/hengyin@4667f716` `chengyinhub-xcx/`.
The iOS baseline is `migration/native-ios@f61b07e`. Branch: `cloud/align-B-20261019`.

**Area B covers:** entry/login, the Home tab, primary navigation (tab bar), 我的 (profile), personal details, favorites, participant info, settings, legal and deregistration pages, and global search.

Priorities:
- **P0**: a user notices immediately. Wrong navigation or structure.
- **P1**: a missing entry point, section or copy on a page that otherwise exists.
- **P2**: visual or detail differences, or something that needs a backend or product decision.

✅ means fixed on this branch.
