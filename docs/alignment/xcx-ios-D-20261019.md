Method: each page's js/`utils/features` client was read for its `/api/...` calls. Those paths and the key zh copy were grepped in iOS `Core/`, `App/` and `Resources/Localizable.xcstrings`. "Absent" means grep found no match; nothing was established at runtime. Priorities:
- **P0**: the page or flow is missing, or core behaviour is wrong.
- **P1**: fields, states or copy are missing.
- **P2**: cosmetic.

Repository rules still apply to anything listed:
- No live mutations, real location sampling, payments, OAuth or SMS.
- Reviewed endpoint allowlists stay as they are.

A gap that needs any of these is marked **policy-blocked**. Its UI and contract can be prepared, but it cannot be enabled from this branch.
